## Delivery checklist

- Claims have source locations and uncertainty is preserved.
- Names, dates, equations and numerical values have been checked.
- Every external asset has a recorded credit and applicable permission/license.
- Captions, alt text and visual hierarchy suit the intended format.
- Platform length, aspect ratio and export requirements are confirmed.
- A human approves the public wording and final asset.

Publication is a separate action from creating a draft. This note tracks approval and results; it does not publish or upload anything.
