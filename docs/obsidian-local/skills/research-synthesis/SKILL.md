# Research Synthesis

## Method
1. State the research question, scope, source set and date limits.
2. Extract each source's title, authors, date/version, method, population/system and relevant page/section.
3. Build a claim-to-source table and separate direct observations from author conclusions and your inference.
4. Compare methods and findings; identify contradictions, missing controls and limits to generalization.
5. List missing literature and the exact search needed before calling the review comprehensive.

## Output
Return `question`, `source table`, `synthesis`, `contradictions`, `limitations`, `evidence gaps` and `next search`.

## Safety
Never invent citations, DOI values, quotations, sample sizes or results. Do not present supplied sources as a systematic review unless the search and inclusion process were actually performed.
